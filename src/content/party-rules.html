PROPOSED DRAFT (v0.1). These Rules and Regulations are made under the proposed Constitution of PGC Nigeria and are subordinate to it, to the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended) and to the Electoral Act. They have not been adopted and must be reviewed by qualified Nigerian legal practitioners. Figures in [square brackets] are proposals.

Part A — Who the Rules bind

  1. These Rules bind every member of the Party who seeks, or holds, an elective public office on the Party's ticket ("a Party candidate" or "a Party office holder"), and every Party officer.
  2. A person becomes bound on registering as an aspirant and remains bound until the end of the tenure for which the person was elected, or, if not elected, until the declaration of the result.
  3. Every aspirant shall sign the Candidate Code and the Governance Mandate in the form published by the Party.

Part B — The Mandate Rules

  1. Predetermined projects only. A Mandate may contain only projects that were recorded in the Development Register before the aspirant registered in the Candidate Register.
  2. Minimum and jurisdiction. A Mandate shall contain at least [three] projects within the jurisdiction of the office, as set by Article 20 of the Constitution. A Mandate may contain more.
  3. Dates. Every Mandate Project shall carry a target completion date within the tenure of the office.
  4. Lawful means. A Party office holder shall pursue Mandate Projects through the budget, appropriation, procurement and oversight procedures the law provides, and shall publish the budget line, the release and the contract for each project when they exist.
  5. Quarterly reports. A Party office holder shall publish a delivery report for each Mandate Project within [thirty] days of each quarter end, with documents. The report shall distinguish between funds allocated, funds released and work verified.
  6. Verification. A project counts as Delivered only after independent verification. A Party office holder shall give verifiers lawful access to information about the project and shall not obstruct them.
  7. Changes. A Mandate Project may be changed only under Article 20(3) of the Constitution, by decision of the Mandate Committee, with published reasons.
  8. Documented obstacles. A Party office holder who cannot deliver a project for reasons beyond the office holder's control shall tell the Mandate Committee in writing, with evidence, within [thirty] days of learning of the obstacle, and shall record the steps taken to overcome it.

Part C — The Candidate Code

Every Party candidate and office holder shall:

  1. obey the Constitution, the Electoral Act and every other law, and respect the courts and all lawful institutions;
  2. campaign peacefully, and not use, threaten or encourage violence, intimidation, thuggery or any armed or uniformed group;
  3. not buy, offer or accept votes, and not give or promise gifts to influence voters or delegates;
  4. not use hate speech, or appeal to ethnicity or religion against any person or community;
  5. make only promises that are within the lawful power of the office sought, and not promise any project outside the published Mandate as if it were certain;
  6. declare assets, liabilities and interests before screening and as the law requires after election, and publish a summary;
  7. disclose every donation and every campaign expense as the Electoral Act requires, and keep campaign funds in an account separate from personal funds;
  8. not use public property, public staff or public funds for campaigning;
  9. declare any conflict of interest in a Mandate Project and not take part in a decision about a contract in which the candidate, a family member or an associate has an interest;
  10. not award a public contract, directly or indirectly, to a company in which the candidate has an interest, except as the law allows and after public disclosure;
  11. respond in good faith to citizens' questions about the Mandate, and hold at least [two] public accountability meetings a year in the jurisdiction after election;
  12. cooperate with the independent assessors, the Ethics Committee and all lawful oversight bodies.

Part D — Screening and primaries

  1. The Screening Committee shall check eligibility, membership standing, asset and interest declarations, the Code, and the Mandate. It shall publish the names of cleared and not-cleared aspirants, with reasons for any refusal that the law allows to be published.
  2. An aspirant who is not cleared may appeal to the Appeals Panel within [seven] days.
  3. Primaries shall follow the Electoral Act and INEC notice requirements. Delegates and voters shall have the published Mandates of all cleared aspirants before they vote.
  4. The Party shall not impose or substitute a candidate except as the law allows.

Part E — Breach, procedure and sanctions

  1. Breach of the Code. A complaint shall go to the Independent Accountability and Ethics Committee, which shall apply Article 23 of the Constitution: written notice, access to evidence, at least fourteen days to respond, a reasoned decision, and appeal within twenty-one days.
  2. Failure to deliver. The Mandate Committee shall hold the hearing and apply Article 21 of the Constitution, including the good-cause protection.
  3. Sanctions. In order of severity: delivery caution; public censure with a recovery plan; suspension of Party privileges and support; denial of the Party's ticket for later terms; suspension or termination of membership.
  4. No removal from office. A sanction never removes a person from public office. The Party shall not demand an undated resignation letter or any similar instrument.
  5. Publication. The decision, the reasons and the sanction shall be published, except where the law protects a complainant or witness.

Part F — Amendment

  1. These Rules may be amended by the National Governing Council on the advice of the Technical and Research Council, and shall be reported to the next National General Assembly. An amendment shall not apply to a Mandate already signed unless the affected office holder agrees.
